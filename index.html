Kareteren

Lake Karateren is a picturesque lake located in the Takhtakupyr district of the Republic of Karakalpakstan. It is known for its beautiful nature, fish, and the ethno-tourist complex on its coast.

Xiva

Khiva is an ancient and historical city located in the Khorezm region of Uzbekistan. It is known for its ancient minarets, madrasahs, and historical monuments of Ichan-Kala.

Lake Qorateren is located near the Borchitov hill in the Taxtako'pir district. The lake stretches for 5-6 km in width and 26 km in length, and its name is related to the fact that when viewed from above, its bottom appears black and deep..
